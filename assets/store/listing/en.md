## Full description (max 4000)

FinanceFlow is the personal finance and budget planner that helps you track expenses, stick to a monthly budget and save more — with artificial intelligence that works for you, without ever seeing your transaction descriptions.

SEE WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES
• Log income and expenses in seconds, with ready-to-use categories
• Dashboard with your balance, this month's spending and how it changes over time
• Charts by category
• Snap a photo of a receipt or invoice and AI fills in the transaction (Pro)

A MONTHLY BUDGET YOU CAN KEEP
• Budget groups with monthly and weekly limits, and alerts before you go over (Pro)
• AI-suggested budget: a limit for every category and group, based on your income, fixed costs, working capital and savings goal (Premium)
• Forecasts of your income, expenses and balance for the coming months, computed on your device (Premium)
• AI insights on your statistics, with concrete suggestions (Pro)

INVESTMENTS
• Record your portfolio and follow the return of each investment (Premium)
• Educational investment tips tailored to your risk profile (Premium)

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
• AI only receives totals that are already calculated — never your transaction descriptions
• Two-factor authentication and fingerprint or face lock
• Password recovery by email, and changing your password signs you out on other devices
• Export or delete all your data at any time

PLANS
• Free: up to 50 transactions a month, dashboard and charts
• Pro: unlimited transactions, custom categories, budget groups, advanced statistics, CSV export, AI insights on your statistics and receipt scanning
• Premium: everything in Pro, plus AI-suggested budget, forecasts, investment tracking and investment tips

See amounts in your own currency (over 100 currencies) and use the app in English, Portuguese, French, German, Italian or Spanish.

Investment tips are educational content and are not financial advice.
